# Publisher scoring source review

Checked 26 October 2026 against the supplied BRIEF-2 and BASC-3/BYI-2 correction instructions.

## BRIEF-2

[PAR parent interpretive report](https://www.parinc.com/docs/default-source/product-resources/brief2-parent-form-interpretive-report-sample-(002).pdf)
confirms clinical T-score interpretation at 60–64 (mildly elevated), 65–69
(potentially clinically elevated), and 70+ (clinically elevated). The supplied
instructions specify “Not elevated” below 60. These are interpretation rules for
an applicable, already verified T-score—not raw-score conversion formulas.

The reference structures keep Self-Report's Task Completion separate from the
parent/teacher-only Initiate, Task-Monitor and Organization of Materials scales.
No scale T-score averaging is used to produce indexes or GEC.

## BASC-3

[Pearson rating-scales report](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/content/dam/school/global/clinical/us/assets/basc-3/basc-3-rating-scales-report-sample.pdf)
shows the direction-dependent clinical/adaptive profiles and the clinical
60/70 and adaptive 40/30 boundaries. The full five-range reference labels are
those explicitly supplied in the user's correction instructions:

| T-score | Ordinary clinical/problem | Ordinary adaptive/strength |
|---|---|---|
| ≤30 | Very low | Clinically Significant |
| 31–40 | Low | At-Risk |
| 41–59 | Average | Average |
| 60–69 | At-Risk | High |
| ≥70 | Clinically Significant | Very high |

Special indices do not inherit these bands. Exact source classifications take
precedence and are retained separately from a reference-rule interpretation.

[Pearson publication summary](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/content/dam/school/global/clinical/us/assets/basc-3/basc3-publication-summary.pdf)
describes multiple norm groups and says hand-scoring worksheets require the
BASC-3 manual. Its item-scoring prose is not a complete item-membership,
polarity, missing-response, composite, or normative lookup specification.

## BYI-2

[Pearson Q-global entry guidance](https://qglobal.pearsonclinical.com/qg/static/Product/sv/BYI-2/BYI-2_Enter_Scores.htm)
explicitly confirms 0 Never, 1 Sometimes, 2 Often and 3 Always, ages 7–18,
and the five independently selectable inventories.

[Pearson sample report](https://www.pearsonclinical.co.uk/content/dam/school/global/clinical/us/assets/beck/BYI-2-Score-Report-Sample.pdf)
shows separate raw/T/cumulative-percent/category fields and the opposite
interpretive direction of Self-Concept. Its example values are not a general
conversion table or a complete set of category boundaries. No universal T=55
category, symptom cutoff for Self-Concept, or overall Beck score is inferred.

## What the public sources do not supply

The public sources checked do not provide complete, form/age/norm-specific
raw-to-T tables, all item keys, missing-response rules, composite conversions,
or validity algorithms. Both uploaded correction documents explicitly state
that proprietary norm tables and item keys are not included.

The application therefore distinguishes **available reference interpretation
criteria** from **administration-specific standardized scoring readiness**.
Reference criteria do not verify a legacy form, translation, numeric encoding,
or response set. They do not rescore records, estimate percentiles/intervals,
or constitute an official-result import workflow. That workflow and locally
computed raw-to-standard scores must not be described as complete.

Original responses, historical score records, and issued reports remain
unchanged. Assignment, submission and View in English remain available.
