That is a sensible adjustment and directly addresses the only recurring payment-method problem visible in the data.

Your new payment structure is:

* **WeChat Pay:** direct QR code
* **Alipay:** direct QR code
* **Credit/debit cards:** Airwallex
* **Airwallex-hosted WeChat Pay and Alipay:** no longer used

This should eliminate the expired Airwallex WeChat sessions. More importantly, the data shows that every submitted Visa payment succeeded, so Airwallex appears reliable for card payments.

There are still several safeguards I recommend:

1. **Collect contact information before displaying any QR code or opening Airwallex.**
   Capture the purchaser’s verified email, name, selected product, amount and school or organisation.

2. **Give each purchase a unique payment reference.**
   Ask customers to include or submit this reference after paying by QR code—for example, `WS-PETE-0907`.

3. **Add an “I have paid” confirmation form beneath each QR code.**
   Collect:

   * Payment method
   * Amount paid
   * Payment date and approximate time
   * Payer’s WeChat or Alipay name
   * Transaction/reference number
   * Screenshot of the payment confirmation
   * Verified email address

4. **Do not grant access solely because the customer clicks “I have paid.”**
   Set the registration to `Payment verification pending` until you or an administrator verifies receipt.

5. **Create Airwallex card intents only after the customer selects “Credit or debit card.”**
   This will dramatically reduce the large number of unused `CREATED` records.

6. **Reuse the same Airwallex intent during card retries.**
   Page refreshes and repeated clicks should not create additional payment intents.

A clean status sequence for RAOS would be:

`Payment option selected → Payment initiated → Verification pending → Paid → Access granted`

For cards, Airwallex can move the registration automatically to `Paid`. For WeChat Pay and Alipay QR payments, it should remain `Verification pending` until confirmed.

The key remaining weakness is reconciliation: standalone QR-code payments may not automatically tell RAOS which customer or workshop the money belongs to. The unique reference, confirmation form and screenshot upload will solve most of that problem.
