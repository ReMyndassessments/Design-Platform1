What to expect
Step 1

Initial conversation
We start with a brief call or message exchange to understand your concerns and determine whether an assessment is appropriate.

Step 2

Intake questionnaire
You'll complete a detailed background questionnaire covering your child's development, health, and school history.

Step 3

Assessment session
Your child meets with our psychometrician in a comfortable, supportive setting. Sessions typically last 2–3 hours with breaks.

Step 4

Report & debrief
You receive a comprehensive written report followed by a debrief session where we walk you through the findings and next steps.

Your child's privacy is protected
All information shared is strictly confidential. Reports are released only to authorised individuals with your written consent. Data is handled in accordance with applicable privacy legislation.

Your Assessment Portal
After your debrief session, you receive a secure email with a link to your personal portal — available in your preferred language. The portal gives you ongoing access to your child's report and support tools.

Frequently asked questions
Does my child need to prepare?

No preparation is needed. We encourage your child to come as they are — the assessment is not a test with right or wrong answers.

Can I be present during the session?

We ask that parents wait nearby while the assessment session takes place, as our psychometrician works best one-on-one. You will be fully debriefed afterwards.

How long does a session take?

Most assessment sessions run between 2 and 3 hours, with regular breaks built in.

Will I receive a written report?

Yes. Every assessment results in a comprehensive written report with findings and practical recommendations.
