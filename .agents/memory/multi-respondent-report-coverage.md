---
name: Multi-respondent report coverage
description: Requirements for comparative reports across RAOS assessment dashboards.
---

Every RAOS report, whether single- or multi-respondent, must print with the student's name and available identifying demographics (date of birth/age, grade, school, primary language, and case ID); show unavailable fields as not recorded rather than inventing values. Multi-respondent reports must also explain what domains mean in everyday functioning, interpret agreement and differences, identify relative strengths, and give practical recommendations. Keep respondent results distinct; compare only matching domains and never pool or average source scores.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires professional, useful comparative narratives for every multi-respondent assessment, not only the commonly used BASC-3 or BRIEF-2 pages, and states that every printed report must identify the student and demographics when it is separated from the case system. A score list or report without identifying details is not usable as a standalone document. CDP is also multi-respondent and has its own dashboard/data path.

**How to apply:** Add a print-only identity header to every report/export path with student name, DOB/age, grade, school, primary language, case ID, and any relevant respondent/assessment date. For each multi-informant domain, describe the skill area, interpret source agreement or differences in context, identify evidence limits, and recommend a concrete support or follow-up action. Audit every dashboard/report path and verify content in both screen and print views.
