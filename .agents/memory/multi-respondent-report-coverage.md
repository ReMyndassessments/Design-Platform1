---
name: Multi-respondent report coverage
description: Requirements for comparative reports across RAOS assessment dashboards.
---

Every multi-respondent RAOS assessment report must explain what the measured domains mean in everyday functioning, interpret the pattern across respondents (including meaningful differences and relative strengths), and give practical recommendations tied to the reported needs. A score list or generic finding summary is not a sufficient narrative. Keep each respondent's results distinct; compare only matching domains and never pool or average source scores.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires professional, useful comparative narratives for every multi-respondent assessment, not only the commonly used BASC-3 or BRIEF-2 pages. A report that repeats score labels without explaining practical meaning does not meet that requirement. CDP is also multi-respondent and has its own dashboard/data path.

**How to apply:** For each domain, describe the skill area, state what convergent or differing source ratings may indicate in context, identify evidence limits, and recommend a concrete support or follow-up action. Audit every dashboard/report path (CDP, generic scored tools, ReMynd Index, product reports, and Chinese Literacy) and verify that the full narrative appears in both the report and print view.
