---
name: RRCA cross-language interpretation
description: Persistent interpretation limits for RRCA sessions administered in multiple languages.
---

Keep every RRCA language phase and passage score separate. A phase stopped before completion is incomplete, not a zero score; report the stopping point and do not invent a total. A high score in one language does not establish age-level ability across languages or explain difficulty in another.

**Why:** RRCA passages are AI-generated and are not validated matched forms or norm-referenced measures, so pooled scores and direct language-to-language conclusions can mislead.

**How to apply:** In examiner screens, stored results, dashboards, and AI narratives, identify passage and response language separately, describe each phase on its own, and recommend further language-specific evidence where needed.
