---
name: Chinese literacy observation scope
description: Product constraints for the teacher-only developmental literacy profile and its reporting.
---

The Chinese Literacy Observation Profile is an educational observation tool for Grades 3–12. One teacher is sufficient; a second teacher is optional and separate. Parent/student questionnaires or individually administered tests must not become prerequisites for its submission or manual professional report.

**Why:** The user supplied an explicit teacher-only specification. This instrument describes contextual difficulty frequency and educational impact; it is not a dyslexia screener or diagnostic assessment.

**How to apply:** Keep descriptive domains separate; never add an overall index, norms, risk categories, diagnostic conclusions, automated support tiers, or cross-teacher averages. Preserve incomplete drafts without inventing missing evidence or NA reasons. English is the source language; Mandarin/Korean must remain explicit English fallbacks until human bilingual review. External Case Portal synchronization is a separately verified dependency, not a simulated successful sync.
