---
name: Parallel teacher form coverage
description: Product decision about giving both teachers the same teacher-rated assessment forms.
---

Every teacher-rated assessment tool should be available independently to Teacher 1 and Teacher 2. Give each teacher the same questionnaire, but keep their assignments, links, answers, and scoring separate. Parent-only and student-only forms do not become teacher forms.

**Why:** The product owner noticed that CDP and Vanderbilt forms reached only one teacher and explicitly asked for identical form coverage for both teachers, not shared responses.

**How to apply:** When adding a teacher-eligible tool through recommendation or product assignment flows, include both teacher slots. For an existing partial case, a teacher-eligible tool may have only a parent assignment or just one teacher assignment; add whichever teacher slots are missing while preserving all existing assignment data.

**Explicit exception:** The Chinese Literacy Observation Profile requires only one teacher; Teacher 2 is optional. Do not auto-repair or expand this instrument into two mandatory teacher assignments. Its uploaded specification explicitly overrides the general parallel-teacher rule.
