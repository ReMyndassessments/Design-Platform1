# Strengthen Your Core assignment picker

In the normal RAOS Add Assignment modal, show the three required Strengthen Your Core forms under a prominent product category at the top, before respondent selection. The forms must be selectable there; a category that only appears in the respondent-filtered list after choosing a respondent is not discoverable enough. Selecting respondents afterward must preserve the user's selected required forms.

**Why:** The assessment lead needs to recognize and assign this product's required forms quickly without searching respondent-grouped lists or guessing which forms belong to the product.

**How to apply:** Keep the category in the existing RAOS workflow. Preserve normal consent gates and per-form respondent compatibility, but never make selecting respondents a prerequisite to seeing or selecting these forms.
