# Strengthen Your Core assignment picker

In the normal RAOS Add Assignment modal, show the three required Strengthen Your Core components and all four optional extension instruments (REFI, RARPS, RFII, RSSC) in prominent product categories. Selecting a product form creates assignments in every respondent slot supported by that form; do not require a separate respondent-type selection first. Keep standard RAOS forms in the existing respondent-first flow.

**Why:** The assessment lead needs to recognize and assign the required and optional product forms directly in the normal RAOS workflow, without searching respondent-grouped lists or treating an AI suggestion as a restriction on administrator judgment.

**How to apply:** Keep this product-specific form-first behavior inside the existing RAOS workflow. Route only to the form's configured respondent slots and preserve the standard parent-consent gate. Do not change the respondent-first behavior for other RAOS forms. Keep other staff limited to the enabled, approved extension; the administrator's manual assignment choice is the explicit override.
