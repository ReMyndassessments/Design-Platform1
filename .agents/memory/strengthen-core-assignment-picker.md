# Strengthen Your Core assignment picker

In the normal RAOS Add Assignment modal, show the three required Strengthen Your Core components and all four optional extension instruments (REFI, RARPS, RFII, RSSC) in prominent product categories before respondent selection. Keep the existing core forms; do not hide or replace them. An administrator can manually select any of the four extensions, independent of the AI suggestion or programme extension toggle. Selecting respondents afterward must preserve all selected Strengthen Your Core forms.

**Why:** The assessment lead needs to recognize and assign the required and optional product forms directly in the normal RAOS workflow, without searching respondent-grouped lists or treating an AI suggestion as a restriction on administrator judgment.

**How to apply:** Keep the categories in the existing RAOS workflow. Preserve the standard parent-consent gate and per-form respondent compatibility, but never make selecting respondents a prerequisite to seeing or selecting these forms. Keep other staff limited to the enabled, approved extension; the administrator's manual assignment choice is the explicit override.
