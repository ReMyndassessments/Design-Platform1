# Strengthen Your Core programme safeguards

Keep Strengthen Your Core non-diagnostic and school-focused. Intake narratives should be de-identified before any AI call; AI suggestions are assistive and require administrator review. Do not create assessment assignments or send invitations until guardian consent and student assent have been obtained and verified. The pathway uses the core instrument and no more than one approved extension.

**Why:** The programme serves minors and handles sensitive educational information. School referral is not consent to assess, and an AI suggestion is not professional approval.

**How to apply:** Preserve these gates when connecting application review to RAOS cases, instruments, reports, or school communications.
