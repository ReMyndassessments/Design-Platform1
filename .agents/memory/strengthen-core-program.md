# Strengthen Your Core programme safeguards

Keep Strengthen Your Core non-diagnostic and school-focused, integrated as an assessment product within the existing RAOS workflow rather than a separate sidebar system. New applications create one shared RAOS inquiry and a pending RAOS case immediately; retain the original application in that case. The standard parent/guardian consent assignment may be sent from the case, but do not create assessment assignments or invitations until consent is verified. Parent/guardian consent is the only consent gate; do not require student assent or show an assent form or checkbox. The product is a focused Tier 2 learner profile and intervention plan: RCEP-CORE plus a structured classroom learning observation and student work/prior-intervention review for every case, then at most one enabled, approved extension.

Only the designated Strengthen Your Core promotional link for schools may create a Strengthen Your Core inquiry. The general RAOS website portal remains the ordinary inquiry route and must not tag, route, or display those inquiries as Strengthen Your Core.

De-identify intake narratives before any AI call. After parent/guardian consent is verified, use the standard RAOS School/Teacher Referral and Parent Intake forms in the same case. Generate or regenerate the case-level Strengthen Your Core analysis only after both are submitted, combining them with the original school application and saved RAOS interviews/follow-up. Use the non-diagnostic educational criteria, not the generic clinical intake analyzer. AI recommends no more than one enabled extension and drafts practical, provisional Tier 2 intervention targets, supports, observable goals, and progress monitoring; it must identify uncollected classroom/work-sample evidence and must not present the draft as a final plan.

**Why:** The programme serves minors and handles sensitive educational information. A school inquiry is not parent consent, and an AI suggestion is not professional approval. The attached product brief defines the scope as a proportionate Tier 2 learner profile and intervention plan, not a diagnostic or abbreviated comprehensive assessment.

**How to apply:** Keep the ordinary website form and endpoint generic. Only the designated promotional intake may set Strengthen Your Core product/source metadata and create the linked case. Use the standard RAOS consent assignment and verification, then the standard school referral and parent intake assignments. Do not generate the case-level tool suggestions until both responses are complete. Keep the SYC AI path separate from ordinary RAOS clinical intake analysis. Require the core RCEP-CORE, classroom observation, and work-sample/history review; let the AI recommend zero or one extension and a provisional intervention draft. Use the existing product dashboard to approve an optional enabled extension before assignment; once an assessment tool is assigned, lock the extension selection.
