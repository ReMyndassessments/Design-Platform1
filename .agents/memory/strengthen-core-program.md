# Strengthen Your Core programme safeguards

Keep Strengthen Your Core non-diagnostic and school-focused, integrated as an assessment product within the existing RAOS workflow rather than a separate sidebar system. New applications create one shared RAOS inquiry and a pending RAOS case immediately; retain the original application in that case. The standard parent/guardian consent assignment may be sent from the case, but do not create assessment assignments or invitations until consent is verified. Parent/guardian consent is the only consent gate; do not require student assent or show an assent form or checkbox. The pathway uses the enabled core instrument and no more than one approved extension.

Only the designated Strengthen Your Core promotional link for schools may create a Strengthen Your Core inquiry. The general RAOS website portal remains the ordinary inquiry route and must not tag, route, or display those inquiries as Strengthen Your Core.

De-identify intake narratives before any AI call. The initial school review and later RAOS regeneration must use Strengthen Your Core's non-diagnostic educational criteria, not the generic clinical intake analyzer. AI suggestions are assistive and require administrator review. Collect follow-up information through the existing RAOS case workflow.

**Why:** The programme serves minors and handles sensitive educational information. A school inquiry is not parent consent, and an AI suggestion is not professional approval. The user explicitly chose parent/guardian consent alone, one integrated RAOS inquiry/case workflow, and a strict source boundary between the promotional link and ordinary website inquiries.

**How to apply:** Keep the ordinary website form and endpoint generic. Only the designated promotional intake may set Strengthen Your Core product/source metadata, create the linked case, and use the educational review criteria. Use the standard RAOS consent assignment, inquiry verification, case follow-up, and product dashboard; keep the approved instrument set to the core tool plus at most one administrator-approved enabled extension.
