# Strengthen Your Core programme safeguards

Keep Strengthen Your Core non-diagnostic and school-focused, and integrate it as an assessment product within the existing RAOS workflow rather than a separate sidebar system. Intake narratives should be de-identified before any AI call; AI suggestions are assistive and require administrator review. Do not create assessment assignments or send invitations until guardian consent and student assent have been obtained and verified. The pathway uses the core instrument and no more than one approved extension.

Only the designated Strengthen Your Core promotional link for schools may create a Strengthen Your Core inquiry. The general RAOS website portal remains the ordinary inquiry route and must not tag, route, or display those inquiries as Strengthen Your Core.

**Why:** The programme serves minors and handles sensitive educational information. School referral is not consent to assess, and an AI suggestion is not professional approval. The user explicitly wants the programme treated as a RAOS assessment product, not a separate admin subsystem, and clarified that general website inquiries are not Strengthen Your Core referrals.

**How to apply:** Put promotional-link review in existing RAOS inquiry surfaces; keep the ordinary website form and endpoint generic. Only the designated promotional intake may set the Strengthen Your Core product/source metadata. Use the RAOS product and case dashboards for approved cases, preserving consent and assent gates.
