---
name: RAOS parent assessment link policy
description: Recipient-facing link expectations when consolidating parent assessment forms
---

Confirmed parent assessment packages must have one stable, shareable portal entry link. If staff explicitly confirm that two unnamed parent assessment groups belong to the same person, consolidate them as one group and retire their other assessment entry URLs. Keep per-form tokens available for questionnaires inside the shared portal. Do not merge groups solely because both have a parent role.

**Why:** The user considers multiple assessment links to one parent unprofessional and explicitly chose to disable older assessment entry URLs. Completed initial referral, consent, and intake forms are a separate stage and must remain usable; changing a portal entry URL must not erase submitted answers.

**How to apply:** When changing parent assessment grouping or sending flows, maintain a single canonical invitation, preserve internal form submissions and initial intake access, and do not invalidate another person's package without explicit recipient confirmation.

Resolve a resend against the final recipient identity, including newly entered contact details. Keep parent and teacher invitations separate even when one person uses the same email for both roles. A package label alone must not authorize changing another identified recipient's contact details.

**Why:** A newly assigned form can join an existing consolidated parent portal when staff enter the parent's email. Its original invitation can then become retired; sending that original link would fail even though the form itself remains accessible inside the correct portal.

**How to apply:** Preserve the established invitation for the confirmed recipient, normalize email matching consistently, and leave other identified recipients unchanged.
